MIT licensed; the licence is in LICENSE beside this file.

Use it by typing `/interview-me` followed by what you want, e.g.
`/interview-me I want to change how my workouts are planned`.
It asks one question at a time, each with its best guess, and ends with a
short brief you confirm before any work starts.
