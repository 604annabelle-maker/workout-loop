# Source

Written for this repository from Thariq Shihipar's "Know your unknowns":
https://thariqs.github.io/html-effectiveness/unknowns/
(examples in https://github.com/ThariqS/html-effectiveness/tree/main/unknowns).

Thariq has not published an official skill for it. The community ports
(hiendinhngoc/unknowns, GreatMark/fable-field-guide-skills,
bozhouDev/finding-unknowns-skills) are aimed at coding; this one is for any
topic, and especially for asking well when you don't yet know the subject.

Use it by typing `/unknown` followed by what you want, e.g.
`/unknown I want my workouts to feel better but I'm not sure what to change`.
